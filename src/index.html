How it Works

From call to
published content.

Step 01

Briefing & Onboarding

A 45-minute call to understand your brand, voice, goals and audience. We fill out an onboarding document together — tone, colours, competitors, themes you want to own.

Step 02

Content Strategy

We define your content pillars, formats and a monthly editorial calendar. You review and request changes before we move to production.

Step 03

Production & Design

Our team creates all content — video, copy and visuals — using your brand guidelines. Every piece goes through internal review before landing in your inbox.

Step 04

Review & Approval

You send feedback — we adjust until it's perfect. Unlimited revisions. We work through a simple approval platform where you comment directly on each piece.

Step 05

Publish & Analyse

We publish for you or deliver everything ready to post. Monthly report with the metrics that matter: reach, engagement, clicks and follower growth.
